Three ways ROS 2 nodes talk

Most ROS 2 programs are built from just these three patterns. Here they are, slowed down.

Topic: a radio station

A publisher broadcasts on a named channel such as /scan. Any node tuned to that exact name hears every message. The lidar doesn't know, or care, who is listening.

Used for streams: sensor data, camera images, speed commands.

Service: ordering at a counter

A client asks one question and waits for one answer, like "spawn a turtle at (2, 2)" or "what is the battery level?". Quick in, quick out.

Used for short jobs that finish right away.

Action: a pizza you track on an app

A client sends a goal, gets feedback while the job runs ("in the oven", "on the way"), and finally a result. It can cancel at any time.

Used for long jobs: navigating to a room, moving a robot arm.

    3. Watch it workAn animation or a step-by-step code tracer shows the idea moving.
    4. Try itIn the practice terminal, the Python playground, a puzzle or a game.
    5. Quick checkQuestions with instant feedback and hints.
    6. RecapThe few things to remember, plus flashcards.
