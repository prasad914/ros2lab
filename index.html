    What is ROS 2?

    ROS 2 stands for Robot Operating System 2. It's a free toolbox of programs that helps the parts of a robot work together and talk to each other.

    Engineers around the world use it to build robots for factories, farms, hospitals and science labs. On ROS2Lab, you learn the same ideas they use, explained in a simple way.
