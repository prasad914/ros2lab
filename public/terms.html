1. About ROS2Lab

ROS2Lab is a free educational project developed by the Robotics and ROS2 lab Channel. It offers an online course in ROS 2 fundamentals, with lessons, practice tools, tests, a personal project and a certificate. Contact: prasadprabhu145@gmail.com.

2. Your account

  • Give true and complete details when you register. The name you give is printed on your certificate.
  • Use one account, for yourself only. Do not share your login or let anyone else take tests or submit work for you.
  • Keep your password safe and tell us at once if you think someone else used your account.
  • If you are under 18, register only with the consent of a parent or guardian, who accepts these terms on your behalf.

3. Access period

  • Each approved registration gives exactly 60 days of access, counted from the moment it is approved.
  • If you finish every lesson of Weeks 1 to 6 (the core course) and score at least 60% in each of their tests within that time, your access stays open for life. Weeks 7 to 9 are an optional introductory experience and are not part of the certificate.
  • In your first registration you may ask once for extra time, up to 30 days. The administrator decides; extra time is not guaranteed.
  • If the course is not finished in time, the registration is cancelled automatically and the course materials close.
  • You may then ask to register again, afresh, at most two times. Each request needs the administrator's approval, starts the course from the beginning and gives 60 days without extra time. Further requests are refused automatically.

4. Honest work

  • Tests and the project must be your own work. Do not use another person, a website or an AI tool to answer test questions, and do not share questions or answers.
  • Do not try to get around the course rules, timers or security, or interfere with the website.
  • Activity during tests (for example leaving the test page) is recorded and may be reviewed.
  • If we find cheating, we may cancel test results, a project mark, a certificate or the account.

  • It is valid for 2 years and can be checked online by its ID or QR code. It may be withdrawn if it was obtained by cheating or with false details.
