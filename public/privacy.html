Privacy notice

Version 2026-11-terms. Please read this before you register. It explains what ROS2Lab stores about you, why, and for how long.

Who runs ROS2Lab

ROS2Lab is a free educational project developed by the Robotics and ROS2 lab Channel. Questions, corrections or complaints: prasadprabhu145@gmail.com.

What we collect, and why

  • Account: your name, email address and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase; we never see it). Why: so you can log in.
  • About you: whether you are studying, have graduated or are working; your college, programme, department and year of passing out (students and graduates), or your institution or company and designation (working people; no year of passing). Why: so your instructor knows who is taking the course and can approve your registration.
  • College ID card photo: Why: only to check your college details. It is deleted as soon as it is checked, and at the latest after 30 days.
  • Course activity: lessons opened and completed, study time, test questions, your answers, timings and scores, and a test activity log (leaving the test page, copy or paste attempts, leaving full screen). Why: to run the course, show your progress and keep tests fair.
  • Final project: the code, report and optional output or video link you submit, and your instructor's marks and feedback. Why: to mark the project for your certificate.
  • Certificate: if you apply for one, it shows your name, your college or workplace details, your registration date, the modules you completed, your test and project marks, the issue date, the 2-year validity and the instructor's signature. Why: anyone who has your certificate ID or scans its QR code (for example an employer you give it to) can open it on ROS2Lab to check that it is genuine and still valid. Without the ID it cannot be found.
  • Visit statistics: Google Analytics counts page visits and the type of device and browser, using cookies. Why: to see which pages help students and to improve the course.

Course access period

Each approved registration gives access to the lessons, tests and project for exactly 60 days from approval. If you finish every lesson of Weeks 1 to 6 (the core course) and score at least 60% in each of their tests within that time, your access stays open for life so you can revise the course whenever you like.

  • In your first registration you may ask once for extra time, up to 30 days. The administrator approves or refuses the request.
  • If the course is not finished in time, the registration is cancelled and the course materials close. Your name, email and college from your first registration are kept on record, together with the number of times you have registered. This short record is kept even if you delete your account, so that the registration limits still apply if you sign up again with the same email.
  • You may then ask to register again, afresh, at most two times. Each request needs the administrator's approval and gives 60 days without extra time. Further requests are refused automatically.
  • A signed certificate stays valid for 2 years from its issue date.

We do not collect your phone number, address, date of birth or Aadhaar.

Never upload Aadhaar, PAN, a driving licence or a passport; only your college ID card is accepted. You may cover your home address, phone number, blood group and any barcode on the card.

We do not use advertising, and we never sell your data or share it with anyone except the Google services named here.

Who can see it

Only you (your own data) and the ROS2Lab administrator; a certificate also to anyone you give its ID to. Data is stored with Google Cloud (Firebase) in Mumbai, India, which processes it for us under its terms.

How long we keep it

  • ID card photo: until it is checked, at most 30 days.
  • Detailed test answers and activity logs: about 6 months after the course ends.
  • Your account, details, progress and project: while you use the course, and at most 12 months after your last access period ends.
  • Certificate records: as long as ROS2Lab runs, so certificates stay verifiable, unless you delete your account.
  • Registration record (your email, the name and college of your first registration, and how many times you registered): as long as ROS2Lab runs, so the registration limits can be applied.

Visitor counter, deleted accounts and usage limits

Visitor counter. While a page is open and visible, your browser sends our server an anonymous signal about once a minute: a random number kept in your browser (ros2lab.vid, not linked to your name), the page address without any query, and whether you are logged in. It only shows the administrator how many people are on the site now and today. No IP address is stored, and these entries are deleted after two days. Clearing this site's data removes the number.

Deleted accounts. When an account is deleted (by you or by the administrator), we keep a short record: email, name, institution, account id, registration and deletion dates, who deleted it and the reason given. Only the administrator can see it. If you register again with the same email, the administrator checks your registration before it is approved.

Usage limits. Some actions have per-account limits to stop abuse.

Your choices and rights

You can see and correct your details on the registration page until they are approved, and by email afterwards. You can delete your account and your data at any time from My account (only the short registration record described above is kept), or ask us by email. You can also ask what data we hold or raise a complaint at the address above.

Children

If you are under 18, please register only with the consent of a parent or guardian, who agrees to the Terms of Use and this notice on your behalf.
