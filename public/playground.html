Playground

Six small games for the first ideas of ROS 2: finding your way in the terminal, topics, services and actions, sourcing a workspace, and your first turtlesim session. Each takes a few minutes and uses the same commands and messages you will type on Ubuntu with ROS 2 Jazzy.

The 15 robot missions after them are locked and marked "Not yet validated": each opens when it has a real game that passed its tests against real ROS 2.

Concept lab

Twenty-seven short animations, grouped by course week. Pick one, press its buttons and read the caption under the picture. The lessons use the same animations.
