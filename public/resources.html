Free resources

The course gives you the essentials. When you want to go deeper, these are the free materials we trust most, in the order we suggest you use them. All links open the original site.

ROS 2 Jazzy

  1. Official ROS 2 Jazzy tutorials

    The official step-by-step path: turtlesim, nodes, topics, services, parameters, actions, launch files, workspaces and your first publisher. Do them in order; each takes 5 to 20 minutes.

    Open Robotics. Repeat Weeks 4 and 5 here on your own Ubuntu.
  2. Using turtlesim, ros2 and rqt

    Your first real ROS 2 session. Every command from the Turtle Commander game works here on a real computer.

    Official tutorial, about 15 minutes.
  3. Articulated Robotics: Getting ready for ROS

    A friendly overview of nodes, topics, packages and workspaces, with clear diagrams and videos. Great for building the big picture.

    Josh Newans. Free articles and YouTube videos.
  4. The Robotics Back-End: What is a ROS topic?

    The radio-station explanation of topics, followed by many short ROS 2 tutorials in Python and C++.

    Edouard Renard. Free articles.
  5. Introducing tf2

    Coordinate frames explained with two turtles: one follows the other. Your bridge to robot models and RViz.

    Official tutorial, after the beginner set.
  6. ROS 2 command cheat sheet

    Every ros2 command and colcon option on one page. Print it and keep it next to your keyboard.

    Canonical, Apache 2.0 licence.

Linux terminal

  1. Linux Journey

    Short, playful lessons on the shell, files, permissions, processes and packages, with quizzes. No sign-up needed.

    Practice after Days 1 and 2.
  2. Ubuntu: the Linux command line for beginners

    The official Ubuntu walk-through of pwd, cd, ls, pipes and sudo.

    Canonical, about 1 hour.
  3. The Linux Command Line (free book)

    A complete, readable book by William Shotts for when you want everything in one place.

    Free PDF, CC BY-NC-ND licence.

Python

  1. The official Python tutorial

    The language from the creators of Python. Chapters 3 to 5 match Days 3 to 5 of the course.

    Python Software Foundation.
  2. CS50's Introduction to Programming with Python

    Harvard's free video course, from functions to classes. Watch the lectures on loops and classes alongside Days 4 and 6.

    Harvard University, free with an optional certificate.

C++

  1. LearnCpp.com

    The best free modern C++ tutorial. Chapters 0 to 8 cover everything you need to read a ROS 2 C++ node.

    Free to read, no account.
  2. cppreference

    The reference to look things up in, such as std::vector or std::make_shared. Not a tutorial.

    Community reference.

Real robots: where to go after Week 6

  1. Modern Robotics (free book and videos)

    Frames, forward and inverse kinematics, and wheeled mobile robots, explained step by step with short videos.

    Free book and course videos.
  2. QUT Robot Academy (Peter Corke)

    Short video lessons on robot arms and mobile robots, for when you want to go beyond the course.

    Free, no account.
  3. ros2_control

    How real robots connect motors to ROS 2: diff_drive_controller and joint_trajectory_controller.

    Official documentation.
  4. MoveIt 2

    Inverse kinematics and collision-free motion planning for real arms.

    Official tutorials.
  5. Nav2

    Navigation for mobile robots: maps, localization, path planning and obstacle avoidance.

    Official documentation.

No Ubuntu computer yet?

  1. The Construct: free Linux, Python and C++ for Robotics

    Practise in a full Ubuntu and ROS environment inside your browser. The three basics courses are free with an account.

    Free plan, sign-up needed.
